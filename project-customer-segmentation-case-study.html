Business Objective

From raw logs to actionable segments

Business problem

  • Large transaction logs without validated customer-level segmentation.
  • No clear persona definitions directly linked to marketing actions.
  • No deployable scoring system for operational use.

Project objective

  • Build an end-to-end segmentation system from ingestion to production API and UI.
  • Validate clustering quality with multi-model, multi-metric evidence.
  • Expose explainable predictions marketers can trust and use immediately.
System Pipeline

Core implementation flow

1. Data + Audit

Ingestion from online_retail_II.xlsx, schema checks, type audit, memory optimization, and quality baseline.

2. ETL + Features

Strict cleaning order, RFM expansion, basket behavior features, outlier capping, log transforms, and scaling.

3. Modeling + Validation

KMeans, DBSCAN, Agglomerative, and GMM comparison with silhouette, CH, DB index, inertia, and bootstrap ARI stability.

4. Serving + Deployment

Explainable FastAPI endpoints, Streamlit marketer dashboard, Docker Compose delivery with reusable model artifacts.

Data + EDA

Quality diagnostics before modeling

Missingness

CustomerID missing: 107,927 rows (20.54%). Description missing: 2,928 rows (0.56%).

Duplicates

6,865 exact duplicate rows (1.3065%) identified and removed to protect frequency/monetary integrity.

Invalid values

Quantity < 0: 12,326 rows. UnitPrice <= 0: 3,690 rows. Canceled invoices removed.

Why EDA mattered

  • Distance-based clustering is sensitive to skew and outliers.
  • Bad customer identifiers break entity-level aggregation.
  • Invalid transactions can distort recency, frequency, and monetary estimates.

ETL + Feature Engineering

Exact cleaning and transformation logic

ETL sequence

  • Remove canceled invoices and non-positive Quantity/UnitPrice rows.
  • Drop invalid/missing CustomerID and cast to stable integer keys.
  • Deduplicate exact rows and enforce numeric/date types.
  • Create TotalSpend and InvoiceBasketSize transaction features.

Customer-level feature space

  • RFM: recency, frequency, monetary.
  • Behavior extensions: avg_basket_size, total_quantity.
  • Robustification: IQR + percentile capping, log transforms, StandardScaler.
  • Readiness gate: missingness, scaling, skew, correlation, and minimum row checks.
Modeling + Selection

Research comparison and production decision

Algorithms benchmarked

  • KMeans (k-grid), DBSCAN (eps/min_samples grid).
  • Agglomerative clustering (k/linkage combinations).
  • Gaussian Mixture Models (components/covariance variants).
  • Bootstrap stability testing with ARI.

Selection outcomes

  • Best silhouette in KMeans grid occurred at k=2 (0.388147).
  • KMeans stability was strong with mean ARI near 0.959654.
  • Production retrain used k=4 for better marketing actionability.
  • Cluster IDs were controlled for stable API contracts.

Deployed Personas

4 operational segments for marketing teams

Champions

  • High frequency, high spend, recent activity.
  • Why it matters: they drive revenue and advocacy.
  • Action: loyalty, VIP perks, referrals, and early access campaigns.

At-Risk Loyalists

  • Historically valuable customers with declining recency and engagement.
  • Why it matters: high-risk revenue leakage if not recovered.
  • Action: win-back offers, personalized reminders, and support follow-up.

Potential Growth

  • Moderate value customers with strong upsell potential.
  • Why it matters: best segment to grow into future Champions.
  • Action: cross-sell bundles, progressive incentives, and education journeys.

New or Passive

  • Low frequency or early-stage and low-engagement customers.
  • Why it matters: biggest activation opportunity at top of funnel.
  • Action: onboarding flows, first-to-second purchase nudges, and low-friction offers.
Explainability + MLOps

How scoring was made usable in production

Explainable API contract

  • Predicted cluster, persona label, and confidence band.
  • Nearest-cluster distance vector and top feature contributions.
  • Feature insights against segment benchmarks.
  • Persona recommendation plan with objective, channel, cadence, and KPI targets.

Serving and UI stack

  • FastAPI endpoints: /, /health, /metadata, /v1/predict, /v1/predict/batch.
  • Streamlit decision interface for single and batch scoring workflows.
  • Two-container Docker Compose setup: segmentation-api (8000) and segmentation-ui (8501).
  • Model and scaler artifacts mounted consistently for reliable inference geometry.

Decision-ready explainability and guidance layer

  • Executive Brief: plain-language summary of who the customer is and what to do next.
  • Why This Segment: key behavioral drivers behind the assignment.
  • Campaign Playbook: objective, channels, cadence, and KPI suggestions.
  • Confidence and Distances: confidence band plus distance-to-cluster transparency.
  • Raw Technical View: transformed features and full model output for analyst validation.

Limits + Next Evolution

Engineering notes and roadmap

Known constraints

  • Unsupervised quality is metric-dependent and needs periodic re-evaluation.
  • Segment drift is expected, requiring a retraining policy.
  • Persona definitions should be continuously validated against campaign outcomes.

Marketing AI Lab expansion

  • Review NLP intelligence (sentiment and aspect extraction).
  • Churn prediction for 30/60/90-day windows.
  • Conversion propensity, dynamic pricing, and anomaly detection modules.
